# E6: time, layers, settings, and phones

Repository: 3gix-earth, branch `grunt`. Contract: `/workspace/docs/design.md` sections 3 item 4, 5 items 5, 6, 8, and the layout rules of section 5. Builds on E5.

## What to build

**0. E5 follow-ups (do these first)**

- **Inspect over the search box.** The inspect message (E2) opens at the top center, on top of the search box, so a click on the view hides search until it closes. Place it where it covers no control (for example bottom center above the readout on wide screens, and above the bottom sheet on narrow ones).


1. **Time of day** (section 5 item 5): slider over the current local day at the camera's longitude, Now, play and pause, play speed (1x, 60x, 600x, 3600x).
2. **Layers** (item 6): each layer from `layers()` with a switch, and the compute budget (0 to the maximum the renderer reports).
3. **Settings** (item 8): HUD, mode, debug panel.
4. **Layout**: below 600 px wide, the readout, time, layers, settings, bookmarks, and debug panel move into one bottom sheet (`SwipeableDrawer` with tabs); search, compass, and mode toggle stay on screen; nothing covers more than the bottom third of the view while the sheet is closed. Above 600 px, floating panels as before. Safe area insets respected.
5. A pass over every control for touch targets of at least 44 px and keyboard focus.
6. Tests: the slider maps to the right UTC instant for a given longitude; layer switches call `setShown`; the layout switches at 600 px (jsdom `matchMedia` mocked).
7. README: a short guide to the controls.
