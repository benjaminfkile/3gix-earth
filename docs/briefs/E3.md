# E3: Earth mode

Repository: 3gix-earth, branch `grunt`. Contract: `/workspace/docs/design.md` section 4 (common rules and Earth mode). Builds on E2.

## What to build

1. `src/nav/` with a small input layer over pointer events on the view's container (mouse, touch, and pen through one path; `touch-action: none` on the view), and a navigation controller that owns the camera: it reads the pose from the `camera` event and writes with `setCamera`.
2. Earth mode, every gesture of section 4 for mouse and touch: pan keeps the grabbed ground point under the pointer (`raycast`, falling back to the ellipsoid), zoom toward the point under the pointer or the pinch midpoint, tilt and turn around the screen center's ground point, double click and double tap fly toward the point (animated, about 0.8 s, easing).
3. Ground clearance and speed by height (section 4 common rules) applied to every move.
4. Pure functions for the math (pan, zoom toward a point, orbit, clearance) with unit tests; a test that a pan of zero pixels moves nothing; a test that no gesture can put the camera below the ground plus 2 m.
5. A drag that moves less than 4 px is a click or tap and goes to inspect (E2), not to pan.

## Done when

- `npm run ci` prints `ci ok`.
- The summary gives the commit and what you could not check (feel in a real browser, real touch).
